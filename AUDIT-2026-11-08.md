**Real and working (FACT):**

- Multi-tenant schema with `orgId` on essentially every table (`src/db/schema.ts`), UUID PKs, `decimal(14,2)`/`(12,2)` money columns, unique document numbers (`schema.ts:1326-1328`).

## D. Defect register

### D.1 CRITICAL — stop-ship

**C1. `reverseTransaction` posts another tenant's journal entry into the caller's org**
`src/lib/accounting/posting-engine.ts:145-149` selects the original JE by `id` only; the reversal is then posted with the caller-supplied `orgId`. Lines are copied from the foreign entry, so the reversal *balances inside the victim's chart of accounts* while being stamped with the attacker's org.
`[FACT]` no `orgId` predicate. `[INF]` any caller who can reach a reversal action with a foreign JE id corrupts two ledgers and can zero out a victim's balances. Also, the status update at `:178-181` has no org filter. **Fix:** `and(eq(id), eq(orgId))`, throw on mismatch, assert `original.orgId === orgId`, and reverse inside the same transaction as the source document update.

**C2. Payment gateway callback accepts unsigned requests and never posts the payment**
`src/app/api/payments/callback/route.ts:27-52`: the branches are `salt && receivedHash`, `!salt`; when a salt **is** configured but `pp_SecureHash` is **absent**, no branch matches and the request falls through. `:64-73` updates a `paymentTransactions` row if it exists — and does nothing if it doesn't. There is no `postTransaction`, no invoice `receivedAmount`/`balanceAmount` update, no customer-balance change.
`[FACT]` bypass and absence of posting. `[INF]` anyone can mark a transaction "completed" by POSTing without a hash; and even legitimate completions leave AR untouched, so the ledger and the gateway permanently disagree. **Fix:** fail closed when salt is set and hash missing; wrap update + payment record + JE + invoice allocation in one transaction.

**C3. `updatePurchaseOrder` deletes another org's PO items before checking ownership**
`src/lib/actions/purchases.ts:1077` `db.delete(purchaseOrderItems).where(eq(purchaseOrderId, id))` runs before the org-scoped update/verification at `:1098`. All of it is on the global `db`, not a transaction.
`[FACT]` ordering and missing `orgId` on the delete. `[INF]` any authenticated user who guesses a PO UUID wipes its lines; if the subsequent update fails, the PO is left with zero items. **Fix:** resolve the PO with `orgId` first, then do delete+insert+update inside `db.transaction`.

**C4. Any sales invoice with tax > 0 cannot be approved**
The only invoice-creation UI hard-codes `lineTotal: "0"` and never recomputes it (`src/app/(dashboard)/sales/invoices/new/page.tsx:337-338, 385`). Approval computes tax per line from `item.lineTotal` (`sales.ts:1056-1063`), so tax lines are zero, while AR is debited with `invoice.netAmount` which *includes* tax (`sales.ts:1073-1078`). `postTransaction` throws on imbalance (`posting-engine.ts:50-56`).
`[FACT]` the three code facts. `[INF]` deterministic throw → `approveInvoice` returns generic "Failed to approve" (`sales.ts` catch block) for every taxed invoice; the invoice is stuck in draft forever. This is the single highest-impact bug in the product. **Fix:** compute `lineTotal` server-side and store it; never trust the client; derive tax from the same computation used for `taxAmount`.

**C5. Any purchase invoice with tax or shipping cannot be approved**
`purchases.ts:456` debits inventory with `gross - discount` (excludes tax, excludes shipping); `:501` credits AP with `netAmount` (includes tax + shipping); tax debit lines are also computed from `lineTotal` (`:460-464`) so they are zero from the UI. Additionally, when `lineTotal` is non-zero, `resolveAccount(tx, orgId, "tax_receivable")` / `"input_tax"` (`:467-473, 495`) throws because **neither subType exists** in the chart of accounts seed (`accounts.ts:214-250`; only provincial `tax_receivable_srb/pra/kpra/bra` exist).
`[FACT]` all four. `[INF]` every taxed/shipping purchase fails approval — or, if line totals were ever correct, fails one line earlier on a missing account. **Fix:** add a GST input account + `tax_receivable` subType, add a shipping/freight-in account, debit `taxTotal` and shipping explicitly, and assert `Dr = Cr` in a unit test.

**C6. Line-level discounts never reduce gross/net/AR**
`sales.ts:825-843`: `computedGross` sums `qty × price` with no per-line discount; `computedDiscount` comes only from `data.discountAmount` (global). The invoice total therefore ignores every line discount entered by the user.
`[FACT]`. `[INF]` systematic revenue and receivable overstatement equal to the sum of line discounts; customers are billed more than quoted; FBR invoices over-declare value. **Fix:** `taxable = qty*price*(1-discPct/100)`, store `lineTotal = taxable`, and build gross/net from stored lines.

**C7. The balance sheet the UI uses does not balance**
`src/app/(dashboard)/reports/balance-sheet/page.tsx:9,23,41` calls `getBalanceSheetReport` (`reports.ts:262-362`), which (a) excludes income/expense accounts entirely, so current-year net income never reaches equity, and (b) applies `Math.abs()` to every liability and equity balance (`:333-343`), converting credit balances to positive and erasing contra/negative balances. The correct implementation, which sweeps net income into `current_year_pl`/retained earnings, exists at `src/lib/accounting.ts:202` and is used only by tests (`w2-gate.test.ts:252`).
`[FACT]`. `[INF]` after any profitable month, Assets ≠ Liabilities + Equity by exactly the net income; a negative equity org shows positive equity. **Fix:** point the page at `getBalanceSheet`, delete the duplicate.

**C8. Sales tax is never credited for UI-created invoices**
Even after C4 is fixed, tax posting depends on stored `lineTotal`. Because it is always `"0"`, no `tax_payable` line is ever created. AR still includes tax (via `netAmount`), so the liability is missing from the GL while the customer is debited for it.
`[FACT]` `sales.ts:1102-1112` only pushes tax lines when `amount > 0`. `[INF]` perpetual understatement of sales-tax payable; tax returns and GL diverge. **Fix:** same server-side computation as C4; add an invariant test "sum(tax lines) == invoice.taxAmount".

**C9. Remaining cross-tenant writes**
- `purchases.ts:1267-1270`: GRN creation sets `purchaseOrders.status = 'delivered'` by id with no `orgId` — a crafted `purchaseOrderId` marks a foreign PO delivered.
- `sales.ts:738-747`: credit-limit check selects `customers` by id with **no `orgId`** → a foreign customer's balance/limit is read (information disclosure) and the check can be bypassed by id collision/guessing.
- `consolidation.ts:55-120`: `linkChildOrg` accepts an arbitrary child org id with no proof of ownership → attacker links a victim org and pulls its reports.
`[FACT]` all three predicates. `[INF]` cross-tenant integrity and data exposure. **Fix:** add `orgId` to every predicate; validate child org membership against `organizations.parentOrgId` **and** a caller-is-admin check.

**C10. Tax compliance is fabricated**
`fbr-api.ts:72-86` returns success with a fake ID when no key. `tax-returns.ts:73-93` includes **cancelled** purchases (`NOT IN ('draft','revised')`) and computes `totalSales` from `netAmount` (tax-inclusive) at `:55-67`. `reports.ts:2747-2748` computes the WHT annual return over `Jan 1 – Dec 31` instead of the July–June fiscal year.
`[FACT]`. `[INF]` filed returns overstate sales, claim input tax on cancelled bills, and WHT certificates are attributed to the wrong tax year — each is an audit finding with penalty exposure. **Fix:** hard-fail FBR submission when unconfigured; exclude cancelled; use `grossAmount`/taxable value; parameterize fiscal year.

## G. Security and tenant isolation

**Good (FACT):** Clerk everywhere; middleware protects `(dashboard)`; API rate limiting (30/60s/IP); CSRF with constant-time compare for state-changing routes; webhook secrets masked in logs; uploads moved to `.uploads` behind an authenticated route; most server actions call `getCurrentOrgId()` and filter by it; `postTransaction` scopes all lines by org.

**Bad (FACT):**

1. **C1** — reversal reads a JE without `orgId`, writes it into the caller's org.
2. **C3** — PO item deletion without `orgId`, outside a transaction.
3. **C9** — GRN PO status update, credit-limit customer read, `linkChildOrg` all unbound to caller's org.
4. **C2** — signature verification is skippable by omitting `pp_SecureHash` while a salt is configured.
5. **H17** — `getCurrentOrgId` trusts a `profiles` row and ignores `isActive`; a deactivated user keeps full access; one-org-per-user forecloses accountant/multi-client use.
6. **RBAC is inconsistent:** `approveRequest` has no role check; `updatePurchaseOrder` has no role check; role strings are scattered (repo's plan concedes this at `PART_B:48`).
7. **Audit log is not tamper-evident** — `auditLogs` rows are ordinary rows, mutable by any admin path.

**Threat model consequences `[INF]`:** a single authenticated low-privilege user (e.g., `staff`) can (a) destroy another tenant's PO lines, (b) mark another tenant's PO delivered, (c) read another tenant's customer credit data, (d) forge a payment callback. None require admin.

## I. What not to build (yet)

1. **More AI FTEs / agents** — the MCP server returns SQL text; the reports it reads are wrong. Fix the substrate first.
2. **Budgeting & forecasting** — meaningless on a P&L whose gross profit equals net profit.
3. **Multi-currency** — `exchangeRate` is stored and ignored; adding UI would create a second wrong number.
4. **Consolidated reporting UI** — links are IDOR-prone and there is no elimination logic.
5. **Marketplace / partner portal** — 20+ modules with unposted documents will multiply support load.
6. **Custom role builder** — there is no permission matrix to build on; do central RBAC first.
7. **More provincial tax forms** — you cannot compute one correctly today (H18).
8. **Native mobile apps** — PWA first (the repo's own plan agrees, `PART_B:35`).
9. **E-invoicing "compliance badge" marketing** — until FBR submission is real, this is misrepresentation.

## J. Per-domain recommendations

**Sales / AR** — Move all totals to the server; treat the client payload as untrusted input (`qty`, `price`, `discPct`, `taxRate` only). Store `lineTotal` as a computed column at write time. Add a `payments` allocation table (invoice ↔ payment ↔ amount) instead of `invoices.receivedAmount` so partial/multi-invoice payments reconcile. Cancel must reverse, not delete.

**Purchases / AP** — Add `paidAmount`/`balanceAmount` to `purchase_invoices`; build a real AP subledger. Add GST input + freight-in accounts and `tax_receivable` subType. Make PO/GRN/bill a single transactional chain with org-scoped status transitions.

**Inventory** — Route every stock mutation through one helper that writes `products.currentStock`, `warehouseStock`, `batches`, and `stockMovements` in the same tx; ban `Math.max(0, …)`. Seed `inventory_adjustment_income`. Make valuation a single service used by reports and tests.

**Banking** — Persist `currentBalance` from JEs (derived, not stored, or updated in the same tx). Reconciliation should be server-verified: matched lines net to zero, statement balance derived from lines. Keep the provider interface; implement one real CSV/OFX importer before any API.

**Payroll** — Model statutory rules as data (EOBI 5%, PF, slab-based income tax on taxable salary, not basic). Payslip credit account must come from the payment method. Add a payroll reconciliation report (gross → deductions → net → payable accounts).

**Tax** — One `TaxComputationService` used by invoices, purchases, returns, and reports. Hard-fail FBR when unconfigured. Returns must exclude cancelled, use taxable values, and key on `issueDate`. Fiscal-year parameter everywhere (WHT, returns, aging).

**Reports** — Delete duplicate implementations; one canonical service per statement. Add reconciliation invariants as tests: BS balances, TB debits = credits, cash-flow net == cash delta, AR aging == GL AR. Push aggregation into SQL and paginate.

**POS** — Server computes expected cash from shift sales; cash variance posts to a cash-over/short account; stock movements mandatory; offline queue only after online correctness.

**Manufacturing** — BOM exists; ensure WIP/consumption JEs are posted through `postTransaction` and that finished-goods valuation uses the same cost engine.

**CRM / Portal** — Fine as CRUD; add org-scoped tests for portal token access before marketing it.

**AI / MCP** — Replace the stub with a read-only Postgres role exposing org-scoped views; every tool must take and enforce `orgId`. Ground answers in canonical reports only.

**Platform** — Central `can(user, action, resource)` permission matrix, immutable audit log, background queue, and a CI gate that runs `tsc`, lint, unit + PGlite integration on every PR.

### Bottom line

The engineering intent here is good and often above average — a single posting engine that enforces balance, real DB tests, org-scoped queries in most places. But the system currently **cannot approve a taxed invoice, cannot approve a taxed purchase, posts no tax liability, leaves payments unposted, reports a balance sheet that doesn't balance, and fabricates FBR success**. Two write paths cross tenant boundaries. Until C1–C10 are closed and proven by tests, this codebase should not be pointed at a real company's books, and no AI or enterprise feature should be built on top of it.
