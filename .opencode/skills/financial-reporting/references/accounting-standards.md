# Accounting Standards (Double-Entry)

## Core Principles
1. Every transaction affects at least 2 accounts
2. Total debits must equal total credits
3. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
4. Revenue - Expenses = Net Income

## Account Types
- Assets: Debit increases, Credit decreases
- Liabilities: Credit increases, Debit decreases
- Equity: Credit increases, Debit decreases
- Revenue: Credit increases, Debit decreases
- Expenses: Debit increases, Credit decreases

## Journal Entry Rules
- Debit first, then credit
- Indent credit entries
- Include date, description, amounts
- Reference supporting documents

## Common Entries
### Sale Invoice
```
Dr. Accounts Receivable
    Cr. Sales Revenue
    Cr. Tax Payable
```

### Purchase Invoice
```
Dr. Purchases
Dr. Tax Receivable
    Cr. Accounts Payable
```

### Payment Received
```
Dr. Bank/Cash
    Cr. Accounts Receivable
```

### Payment Made
```
Dr. Accounts Payable
    Cr. Bank/Cash
```
