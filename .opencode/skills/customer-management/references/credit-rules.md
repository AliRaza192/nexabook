# Credit Rules

## Credit Limit Management
- Default limit: Rs. 100,000 (configurable per customer)
- Warning threshold: 80% of limit
- Hard stop: 100% of limit (override with approval)

## Credit Terms
- Cash: Payment upfront
- Net 15: Pay within 15 days
- Net 30: Pay within 30 days (default)
- Net 60: Pay within 60 days
- Custom: Configurable

## Credit Scoring
Factors:
- Payment history (on-time %)
- Average order value
- Length of relationship
- Outstanding balance

## Overdue Handling
- 1-30 days overdue: Email reminder
- 31-60 days overdue: Phone follow-up
- 61-90 days overdue: Formal notice
- 90+ days overdue: Block new orders, consider collection

## Credit Hold
When credit limit exceeded:
- Block new invoice creation
- Allow payment receipts
- Require approval for override
- Log override reason
