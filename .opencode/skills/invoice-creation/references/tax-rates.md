# Pakistani Tax Rates (FY 2025-26)

## General Sales Tax (GST)
- Standard rate: 17%
- Reduced rate: 10% (specific goods)
- Zero-rated: 0% (export goods)
- Exempt: 0% (specific items)

## Withholding Tax (WHT)
- On contractor payments: 10%
- On professional services: 15%
- On rent: 10%
- On commission: 10%
- On interest: 10%

## Federal Excise Duty (FED)
- On services: varies by province
- On specific goods: as notified

## Provincial Sales Tax
- Sindh (SRB): 5-8% on services
- Punjab (PRA): 5-8% on services
- KP (KPRA): 8-10% on services
- Balochistan (BRA): 5-8% on services

## Income Tax Slabs (Individual)
- Up to Rs. 600,000: 0%
- Rs. 600,001 - 1,200,000: 5%
- Rs. 1,200,001 - 1,800,000: 10%
- Rs. 1,800,001 - 2,500,000: 15%
- Rs. 2,500,001 - 3,500,000: 17.5%
- Rs. 3,500,001 - 5,000,000: 20%
- Above Rs. 5,000,000: 22.5%

## EOBI Rates
- Employee contribution: 1% of minimum wages
- Employer contribution: 5% of minimum wages
- Current minimum wages: Rs. 32,000/month
