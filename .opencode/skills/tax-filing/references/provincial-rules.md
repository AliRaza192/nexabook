# Provincial Tax Rules

## Sindh Revenue Board (SRB)
- Sales tax on services: 5-8%
- Filing: Monthly by 15th
- Registration required for service providers
- Returns in SRB prescribed format

## Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA)
- Sales tax on services: 5-8%
- Filing: Monthly by 15th
- Registration required for service providers
- Returns in PRA prescribed format

## KP Revenue Authority (KPRA)
- Sales tax on services: 8-10%
- Filing: Monthly by 15th
- Registration required for service providers
- Returns in KPRA prescribed format

## Balochistan Revenue Authority (BRA)
- Sales tax on services: 5-8%
- Filing: Monthly by 15th
- Registration required for service providers
- Returns in BRA prescribed format

## Key Rules
- Register in province where service is provided
- File returns even if no sales (nil return)
- Keep records for 5 years
- Input tax credit on provincial purchases
