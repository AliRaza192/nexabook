# Filing Deadlines

## FBR Deadlines
- Sales Tax Monthly: 18th of next month
- Sales Tax Quarterly: 18th after quarter end
- WHT Monthly: 15th of next month
- Income Tax Annual: September 30

## Provincial Deadlines
- SRB (Sindh): 15th of next month
- PRA (Punjab): 15th of next month
- KPRA (KP): 15th of next month
- BRA (Balochistan): 15th of next month

## Reminder Schedule
- 7 days before: First reminder
- 3 days before: Second reminder
- 1 day before: Urgent reminder
- On deadline: Final warning

## Penalties
- Late filing: Rs. 10,000 per day
- Non-filing: Rs. 50,000 minimum
- Incorrect return: 10% of tax payable
