# Provident Fund Rules

## Contribution Rates
- Employee: 8.33% of basic salary
- Employer: 8.33% of basic salary (matching)
- Total: 16.66% of basic salary

## Example
```
Basic Salary: Rs. 100,000
Employee PF: Rs. 8,333
Employer PF: Rs. 8,333
Total PF: Rs. 16,666
```

## Vesting Period
- Full vesting: 5 years of service
- Partial vesting: 0-5 years (graduated)

## Withdrawal Rules
- On resignation: Withdraw full amount after vesting
- On retirement: Withdraw full amount
- On death: Nominee receives full amount
- Loan: Up to 50% of balance, 2% interest

## Tax Treatment
- Employee contribution: Tax-deductible
- Employer contribution: Taxable as benefit
- Interest earned: Taxable
- Withdrawal: Taxable if before 5 years

## Record Keeping
- Monthly statement to employee
- Annual statement with interest
- Nomination form mandatory
- Update on salary change
