# Income Tax Slabs (Individual - FY 2025-26)

## Taxable Income Slabs

| Annual Income (Rs.) | Tax Rate |
|---------------------|----------|
| Up to 600,000 | 0% |
| 600,001 - 1,200,000 | 5% of excess over 600,000 |
| 1,200,001 - 1,800,000 | Rs. 30,000 + 10% of excess over 1,200,000 |
| 1,800,001 - 2,500,000 | Rs. 90,000 + 15% of excess over 1,800,000 |
| 2,500,001 - 3,500,000 | Rs. 195,000 + 17.5% of excess over 2,500,000 |
| 3,500,001 - 5,000,000 | Rs. 370,000 + 20% of excess over 3,500,000 |
| Above 5,000,000 | Rs. 670,000 + 22.5% of excess over 5,000,000 |

## Monthly Calculation
```
Annual tax = slab calculation
Monthly deduction = Annual tax / 12
```

## Adjustments
- Tax credit for zakat: full amount
- Tax credit for donations: up to 30% of taxable income
- Minimum tax: 1% of turnover (if applicable)
