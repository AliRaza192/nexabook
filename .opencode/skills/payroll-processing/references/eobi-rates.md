# EOBI Rates (FY 2025-26)

## Employee Contribution
- Rate: 1% of minimum wages
- Current: Rs. 320/month
- Maximum: Rs. 320/month

## Employer Contribution
- Rate: 5% of minimum wages
- Current: Rs. 1,600/month
- Maximum: Rs. 1,600/month

## Total Monthly
- Employee: Rs. 320
- Employer: Rs. 1,600
- Total: Rs. 1,920

## Who Pays
- Employee deduction from salary
- Employer adds to cost
- Both reported in EOBI return

## Exemptions
- Government employees
- Armed forces
- Foreign diplomats

## Registration
- Mandatory for organizations with 5+ employees
- Register within 30 days of hiring
- Monthly return by 15th
